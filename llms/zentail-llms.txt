# Zentail


> Zentail is a multichannel ecommerce platform that helps brands manage product listings, inventory, pricing, and orders across marketplaces like Amazon, Walmart, Target Plus, and eBay from one system.


## Overview


- [Zentail homepage](https://www.zentail.com/): Overview of the platform and how it manages listings, inventory, and orders across marketplaces.
- [Multichannel overview](https://www.zentail.com/multichannel): How to manage ecommerce operations across multiple marketplaces from a single platform.
- [Pricing](https://www.zentail.com/pricing): View pricing plans, included features, and how Zentail is packaged for multichannel sellers.


## Marketplaces


- [Walmart Marketplace integration](https://www.zentail.com/walmartmarketplace): Create and publish Walmart listings faster with automated setup, real-time validation, and support for Walmart listing requirements.
- [Target Plus integration](https://www.zentail.com/target-plus-marketplace): Manage Target Plus listings with structured product data, required attributes, and centralized control for compliance.
- [Amazon integration](https://www.zentail.com/amazon): Create and manage Amazon listings, sync inventory, and update pricing using a single catalog.
- [All marketplace integrations](https://www.zentail.com/apps): List of supported marketplaces and integration partners.


## Platform


- [Multichannel platform](https://www.zentail.com/multichannel): Manage listings, inventory, and orders across all marketplaces from one system instead of separate tools.
- [Product data management](https://www.zentail.com/solutions/product-information-management): Organize product data and map attributes to meet marketplace requirements.
- [Listing management](https://www.zentail.com/solutions/listing-management): Create, edit, and publish listings in bulk across multiple marketplaces.
- [Inventory management](https://www.zentail.com/solutions/inventory-management): Keep inventory levels synced across channels to avoid overselling.
- [Order management](https://www.zentail.com/solutions/order-management): View and manage orders from all marketplaces in one place.
- [Pricing management](https://www.zentail.com/solutions/pricing-management): Set pricing rules and update prices across marketplaces from one system.
- [Business analytics](https://www.zentail.com/solutions/business-analytics): View performance data across channels to track sales and identify trends.

## Use Cases


- Manage listings across Amazon, Walmart, and Target Plus from one platform
- Expand from one marketplace to multiple channels
- Keep product data consistent across marketplaces
- Sync inventory to avoid overselling
- Reduce manual work in ecommerce operations


## Disambiguation


**Zentail is NOT:**
- A marketplace
- A fulfillment provider
- A dropshipping supplier


**Zentail IS:**
- A multichannel ecommerce platform for managing marketplace operations
